# Testing Party Trivia: a guide for testers

Thank you for helping test this game. You don't need any technical knowledge. All you need is a web browser on a computer and a phone. The whole walkthrough takes about 10 minutes.

## What you'll receive

You may receive a ZIP download or three working website links.

**If you received the ZIP:** extract the whole ZIP into a folder, then double-click **Start Trivia.bat** (Windows) or **Start Trivia.command** (Mac). The first launch needs internet to prepare the app. A browser start page opens with the four screen buttons below. Keep the launcher’s status window open while testing.

**If you received website links:** open them directly. No download is needed.

Keep the host and grader links private, because they work like passwords.

- **Display link:** the "TV screen". It shows questions, the join QR code and the leaderboard.
- **Host link:** the control panel for running the game.
- **Grader link:** the page for marking typed answers.

Players don't need a link from you. They join by scanning the QR code on the display screen.

**Will phones connect?** If the links are on a website (online hosting), phones can join over Wi-Fi or mobile data. If the game is running on someone's own computer, phones must be on **the same Wi-Fi** as that computer. If you're unsure which setup you have, ask whoever sent the links. Testing on real phones and real networks is part of what we're asking you to check. It hasn't been confirmed yet.

## Setting up your screens

- **On your computer,** open the display, host and grader links in **separate browser tabs**, and switch between them as you go.
- **On your phone,** scan the QR code on the display tab with the camera app. This opens the player page. Enter a nickname and tap **Let's go**.
- **To play from the computer too,** you can open the join address shown on the display in another tab. Note that all tabs in one browser count as **the same player**. To add a second player, use a **private/incognito window** or a **second phone**.

## The 10-minute walkthrough

The game has four sample questions, and you know the answers ahead of time:

| #   | Type            | Question                               | Answer                        |
| --- | --------------- | -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| 1   | Multiple choice | Planet with the most spectacular rings | Saturn                        |
| 2   | True/false      | An octopus has three hearts            | True                          |
| 3   | Short answer    | Capital of France                      | Paris                         |
| 4   | Free response   | Pitch a terrible ice cream flavor      | Anything (graded by a person) |

Try some wrong answers on purpose too.

1. **Join.** With your phone (plus any extra players) joined, check that the names appear on the display and in the host's Players list.
2. **Start.** On the host tab, press **Start game**, then **Open question**.
3. **Question 1 (multiple choice).** Before answering, check the countdown on the display, host and phone. Press **Pause** on the host. Check that the timer freezes everywhere, then press **Resume** and answer on the phone. Once everyone has answered or time runs out, the answer is shown.
4. **Continue.** On the host, press **Show leaderboard**, then **Next question**. Use these two buttons to move on after each question.
5. **Question 2 (true/false).** **Refresh the page on your phone** while the question is open, then answer it. You should come back as the same player without joining again.
6. **Question 3 (short answer).** This starts a new round, so press **Open question** again. Type "Paris" (or "paris", or a wrong answer), then press **Send** before time runs out. When everyone has answered or time is up, go to the grader tab. Correct answers may already be marked automatically. Mark the rest **Correct** or **Wrong**, then press **Submit & reveal**.
7. **Question 4 (free response).** Write a flavor pitch and press **Send**. In the grader, try **Correct**, **Partial** and **Wrong**. Press the star (**Feature on projector**) on an answer you like. It should stay hidden on the display until you press **Submit & reveal**, then appear with its author’s name.
8. **Finish.** Press **Show leaderboard**, then **Final results** on the host. Then press **Restart game**. Scores should go back to zero while players stay joined.

Other things to try if you have time: close the phone browser and reopen the link, lock the phone for a minute, **Skip question**, **End game**, and adjusting a player's score on the host.

## Reporting a problem

For anything that looks wrong, confusing or slow, write down:

1. **Device and browser:** for example, "iPhone 13, Safari" or "Windows laptop, Chrome".
2. **Screen:** display, host, grader or player phone.
3. **What you did:** for example, "pressed Pause during question 1".
4. **What you expected**, and **what actually happened**.

Screenshots help a lot. Also say which question you were on and how you were connected (Wi-Fi or mobile data).
